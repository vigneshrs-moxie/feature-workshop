## What you get at the end

- **Levels replace flows.** New job sheet and job menu offer L0 Tweak · L1 Small · L2 Standard · L3 Large, each with a one-line "what runs" hint. `options.json` becomes schema 2 with `level` and `models`, still carrying every v1 field so old skills keep working.
- **A level is suggested from two sources on one card** (`LevelCard`): the rules (description scan, later `git diff --stat`) and the Foreman's first brief (`suggested_level` + reason in `status.json`). You confirm or change; overrides are audited.
- **Gate cards** (`GateCard`) pinned above the composer when the Foreman waits for a decision: the brief's questions (up to three), **Approve** with its effect named ("Approve plan v2 and start Build"), **Edit, then approve**, **Revise with feedback**, **Other guidance**, **Approve, start after reset** (usage-limit aware), and **Stop job** behind ⋯. The compose path finally sends real answers, verdicts and the "At these versions" line, as the classic page does.
- **"Also decided" list** from `decided_on_your_behalf`, each with one Override link that flips it to "changed" with a note.
- **Next-step chips** on the gate card: *Skip next* · *Lighter* / *Heavier* · *Ask Fable* · *I'll do this part* · *L↑/L↓*. Each patches `options.json` and adds one visible line to the reply you are about to send (not a separate send, so no 409 while a run is active and the brief's "answered" state stays right).
- **Safeguards:** a card remembers the spec and artifact versions it was raised for and greys out with "Plan changed since this was proposed" if they move; a gate untouched for 4 hours notifies you and parks the job in Idle. Nothing is ever auto-approved.
- **Job drawer** with `notes.md` (read and write) and the options editor, so the classic Options page is no longer needed.
- **Review rounds 1–3.** `review_rounds` accepts 3 in the dashboard and the skill.
- **Skill update in the public repo** (`claude-feature-workflow`): Levels table, `level` field, L0 gate-free wording, `suggested_level`, chip semantics, models per level. Shipped so new dashboard + old skill and old dashboard + new skill both still work.
- **Demo covers it all:** the fake Foreman reads `level`, writes `suggested_level`, raises a plan gate with questions, decisions on your behalf and versioned artifacts, and reacts to chips.
- **Hints for teammates:** hover hints on every new control, a "How this works" fold on the gate card and the drawer, and the Levels explanation on the New job sheet.

## Decisions for you

- **D1 L0 authorization text → rec:** at L0 the human's original request plus `level: 0` in `options.json` counts as the build go-ahead, for one build only. The skill says so in one named paragraph in `job-options.md`, cross-referenced from `workflow-gates.md` and `worker-rules.md` so the implementer does not refuse for lack of a quoted yes. Everything on the "still asked separately" list stays asked. Risk triggers raise the level and bring back the plan card. *Alternative:* keep a one-click "Go" card at L0. Simpler to explain, but it is a gate, and L0 was decided gate-free.
- **D2 Chip semantics → rec:** one line each in the reply, in words the skill learns:
  - *Skip next*: add the next stage to `skip` for this job.
  - *Lighter* / *Heavier*: for the next stage only, drop to the single head-inspector lens or add the full lens table; at build, Heavier adds the tester.
  - *Ask Fable*: add the next point to `fable_checks`.
  - *I'll do this part*: the next stage is yours; the Foreman waits in `waiting_for_you` like manual test does today.
  - *L↑/L↓*: change `level`; the card shows the consequence ("L1→L2 adds: tester, permissions + tests lenses"); raising mid-build keeps today's rule (stop and ask which stages to add); lowering never removes an approval still owed.
- **D3 Parked jobs → rec:** no new group. A parked job stays under Needs you with a "parked 4h" chip and an alert; moving it to Idle would hide the ask. *Alternative:* the design doc's Idle parking, as written.
- **D4 Your own `~/.claude` → rec:** run `install.sh` on your machine as part of this phase so your skills are symlinks to the public repo (your copies are plain files from Oct 12–14, backed up by the script). Otherwise the skill edit cannot reach your own jobs.
- **D5 Level at job creation → rec:** the New job sheet shows the four levels with the rules' suggestion pre-selected from the description as you type (no model, instant); the Foreman's suggestion arrives with the first brief and the `LevelCard` shows both. *Alternative:* no choice in the sheet, always wait for the Foreman.
